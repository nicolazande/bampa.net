Takes off on its own.
Flies far.

ARES rises vertically like a helicopter and carries on like a plane. We designed it from scratch, and it is the first of a team of robots, in the air and on the ground, built to reach where nobody can today.

01 — Take-off

It rises like a helicopter.

A patch of grass is all it needs. No runway, no launcher, no net to catch it.

02 — Transition

Then it becomes a plane. Smoothly.

Many VTOLs switch from vertical to forward flight in one abrupt step: lift motors off, pusher on. Not ARES. Its rotors tilt continuously, thrust shifts gradually from lifting to pulling, and the wing takes over the weight bit by bit.

03 — Inside

A real brain.

Autopilot, onboard computer and a camera that reads the ground: ARES knows where it is even when there's no GPS.

04 — Transport

Seven pieces. One carry-on.

It comes apart with a single key and travels with you.

What you see is the real design model, not a fantasy render.

The vision

The first of a team.

The next step is robots working together, in the air and on the ground, without a pilot for every machine. Here's what they'll be able to do.

  • Mountain rescue
  • Emergencies
  • Sites to protect
  • Remote infrastructure
Next step

What flies sees. What's on the ground acts.

ARES explores and draws the map; ground robots use it to reach the points it found. And when they're out of range, it becomes their radio relay.

Next step

It hears other drones.

It listens to the radios of nearby drones and, with several listeners, works out where they are. Without transmitting anything.

Next step

A protected zone, in layers.

Ground sensors and patrol drones together: whoever enters is detected, recognised and reported.

Detecting and reporting, yes. Intervening is up to those authorised by law.
