The thesis

What can't be done today.

Two needs are growing in Europe, and today's products don't meet them.

01

Data can't leave

Subcontractors, medical and defence companies can't upload manuals, drawings and code to the cloud. They want AI, but inside their own walls.

02

There is no signal

In the mountains, in emergencies and where GPS is jammed, a drone and its pilot come to a stop. Real autonomy is needed: robots that find their way and coordinate on their own.

03

The same technical base

Running models locally, robotics, electronics, simulation: the same skills. The AI that works in a company today is the AI that will work on board tomorrow.
