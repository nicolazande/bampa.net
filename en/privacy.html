Data collected

The contact form sends nothing to our server: it prepares a message in your email app, and you decide whether to send it. We process the data you write to us (name, email, company, message) only to reply and for any business relationship that follows.

Like any web server, the host keeps technical logs of IP addresses and requested pages, retained only as long as needed to keep the service secure.

Legal basis and retention

Processing is based on your request to be contacted (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Messages are kept for the duration of the relationship and of legal obligations.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, and object to processing, by writing to info@bampa.it. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
